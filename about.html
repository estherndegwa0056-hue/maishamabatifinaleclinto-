Our Story

About Maisha Mabati

A roofing company built around quality, durability, value and the growing needs of Kenyan construction.

Established 2009

A focus on better roofing for Kenya.

Maisha Mabati was established in 2009 to meet the growing requirements for quality roofing at affordable pricing. It was the vision and commitment of Mr Narendra Raval EBS (Guru) to supply building and construction materials at affordable costs to the local Kenyan population.

Maisha Mabati Mills began with galvanised and coloured steel roofing sheets. The products are focused on being durable, functional, strong and cost-effective to enable Kenyans around the country to build better standard homes.

This commitment and drive led Maisha Mabati Mills Limited to build a state-of-the-art Alu-Zinc plant in 2013 at Ruiru, Kenya. The company supplies Alu-Zinc and colour-coated steel roofing sheets under the Maisha Alu-Zinc brand in local and regional markets.
